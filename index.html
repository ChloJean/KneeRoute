Evidence library

Sources behind the pathway

A production version should show exactly which source supports each rule.

BOASt — Best Practice for Management of ACL Injuries

British Orthopaedic Association, September 2020. Includes an audit standard that acute traumatic knee haemarthrosis should be assessed by a clinician proficient in assessment of knee injuries.

Open official source ↗
BOASt — Assessment and Management of First Time Lateral Patellar Dislocation

British Orthopaedic Association, December 2024. Recommendations for assessment and initial management of FTLPD, including suspected dislocations.

Open official source ↗
BOASt — ACL Injury in the Skeletally Immature Patient

British Orthopaedic Association, May 2022. Recommendations for suspected ACL injury in skeletally immature patients.

Open official source ↗
NICE CKS — Ottawa knee rule

NICE CKS recommends use of the Ottawa knee rule to determine whether radiography is needed for suspected knee fracture in people over 2 years.
